# Charred leek soup

Serves 4. 40 minutes. From the Wren Street community kitchen's winter menu.

- 4 leeks, halved lengthways
- 1 potato, diced
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 100 ml single cream

Char the leeks cut side down in a dry pan, chop them, simmer with the potato in the stock for 20
minutes, blend, stir in the cream.
